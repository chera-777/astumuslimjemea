# ASTU Muslim Connect

🕌 ASTU Muslim Jemea — A Cross‑Platform Community for Muslim Students  
ASTU Muslim Jemea is a holistic cross‑platform application designed to strengthen the academic, spiritual, and social life of Muslim students at ASTU and other Ethiopian universities. Built on Islamic values, the platform integrates portals, resources, governance, communication, group systems, and support programs into one unified ecosystem, ensuring balanced representation, halal boundaries, and meaningful student engagement.

🔐 Registration & Profiles

Registration Form requires: Full Name, ID Number, Phone Number, Department, Batch/Year, Café vs. Non‑Café status, Gender, and University selection.

University Selection ensures each student joins their own university community, with activities visible only inside that community.

Cross‑University Connections allow optional collaboration between universities while keeping each community distinct.

Café Students Only receive automatically generated Ramadan meal cards.

Profile Photos — Students can upload photos, but female students’ profile photos are hidden from male users to maintain halal boundaries.

Verification — ID card scan or admin approval ensures authenticity and prevents fake accounts.

🔐 Multi‑Portal Access  
👑 Amirs’ Portal

Main Sector — Oversees overall leadership, coordination, and community vision.

Qira’at Sector — Manages Qur’an recitation programs, tajweed classes, hifz competitions, and Zikr challenges.

Academic Sector — Provides tutoring schedules, study groups, exam reminders, and academic mentoring.

Financial Sector — Handles donations, sadaqah programs, scholarships, meal card funding, and financial transparency.

Social Sector — Organizes halal social activities, community events, student welfare initiatives, and lost‑and‑found boards.

Gender‑Specific Amirs/Amirahs — Parallel leadership structures for brothers and sisters, ensuring halal boundaries.

📋 Committee Portal  
Election management, announcements, voting oversight, grievance handling, and decision‑making dashboards, with separate committees for brothers and sisters.

👤 Student Portal  
Access to Qur’an, Kitabs, Hadith, Zikr detector, Ramadan calendars, Azan player, tutoring schedules, ders announcements, and Ramadan meal card generation exclusively for café students.

🌐 Guest Portal  
Limited access for external visitors, donors, and community supporters.

📲 App Navigation Overview  
Mobile & Tablet Devices — Bottom navbar with six sections: 🏠 Home, 📖 Resources, 📰 News, 🗳 Governance, 💬 Community, 👤 Profile.
Desktop & Laptop Devices — Sidebar menu for intuitive access to portals, announcements, and analytics.

🧠 Core Features  
📖 Islamic Resources  
Qur’an, Kitabs, Hadith collections, Zikr detector, Ramadan tools (meal cards for café students only, calendars, Azan player).

🗳 Governance & Elections

Elections are conducted annually or as scheduled.

The committee prepares official forms, announces election dates, and manages the process transparently.

Students vote through their portals for each Amir sector (Main, Qira’at, Academic, Financial, Social).

Separate elections for brothers and sisters ensure halal boundaries.

Voting is anonymous and secure, with results automatically tallied and published by the committee upon the deadline.

Fraud detection and identity verification ensure fairness and integrity.

Results are displayed in both student and committee portals, with clear sector‑by‑sector breakdowns.

🎓 Academic & Spiritual Support  
Senior‑freshman tutoring, ders and Hadith session announcements, cancellation notices, and academic reminders.

💬 Communication Tools

Individual Chatting — Every student has their own chat section to message peers.

Group Chatting — Each group of 14 students has its own chat space.

Meeting Features — Live audio/video meetings between Amirs, committees, groups, or the whole community.

Calls — Every student can call others directly, either standard or video calls.

Gender Boundaries — Female students have dedicated communication features that respect privacy and halal rules.

🌍 Inter‑University Connections  
Each university maintains its own community, with optional links for broader collaboration.

🏆 Challenges & Leaderboards  
Zikr and Qur’an hifz competitions, leaderboards, and motivational tracking.

🎥 Multimedia Content  
Video Hadith and lectures from scholars like Omar Suleiman, plus curated educational media.

👥 Group Formation & Roles  
✨ Annual Group System

At the start of each academic year, the system automatically generates diverse groups of 14 students, spanning 1st–5th year.

Each group includes at least two familiar peers to strengthen trust.

Groups are managed under the guidance of Amir sectors, ensuring alignment with community goals.

Every group has its own niqab (identity space) for communication, announcements, and collaboration.

Groups are gender‑segregated (brothers’ groups and sisters’ groups) to maintain halal boundaries.

🎭 Roles Within Groups

Amir/Amirah — Group leader, responsible for vision and coordination.

Naqeeb/Naqeebah — Assistant leader, supporting the Amir/Amirah.

Murshid/Murshida — Mentors guiding spiritual and academic growth.

Brothers/Sisters — Members contributing to learning, projects, and halal social activities.

🌱 Purpose of Groups

Spiritual growth through Qur’an, Hadith, and Zikr programs.

Academic tutoring and project collaboration.

Halal social activities with clear gender boundaries.

Trust‑building and peer support across academic levels.

🚀 Essential University‑Wide Solutions  
📅 Academic Scheduling Integration — Class timetables, exam reminders, assignment deadlines.
📦 Lost‑and‑Found Board — Campus‑wide item recovery system.
🧠 Mental Well‑Being Resources — Non‑medical support, peer mentoring, stress‑relief programs.
💰 Financial Aid & Scholarships — Application tracking and transparent allocation.
📢 Event Calendars & Grievance Reporting — University events, complaint submission, and resolution tracking.
📚 Library & Resource Access — Digital library integration for academic materials.
🏠 Housing & Café Management — Meal card eligibility, café menus, and dormitory notices.
🛡 Anti‑Harassment & Safety Reporting — Anonymous reporting system for misconduct or safety concerns.
📰 Campus News Hub — Centralized news feed for university updates, Islamic events, and student announcements.
💼 Career Services Portal — Internships, job postings, CV workshops.
🚌 Transportation Updates — Bus schedules and campus shuttle info.
🏥 Health Services Integration — Clinic hours and appointment booking.
📊 Exam Results & Grade Tracking — Linked to student profiles.
🤝 Student Union Portal — Broader campus governance and representation.

🤲 Support & Charity  
💸 Donations & Sadaqah Programs — External contributions and student‑led initiatives.
🎉 Community Events — Organized programs to strengthen unity and service.

🔐 Security & Privacy  
End‑to‑end encryption for all student data.
Verified identity for elections, donations, and meal card eligibility.
Fraud detection for financial transactions.
Gender‑Sensitive Privacy — Female students’ profile photos are hidden from male users; communication features respect halal boundaries.
Role‑based access control ensures brothers and sisters’ data remain separate.
Emergency alert system for safety incidents.

💸 Sustainability & Growth Strategy  
Community‑driven donations and sadaqah.
Partnerships with student unions, Islamic organizations, and local businesses.
Expansion to other Ethiopian universities with localized features.
